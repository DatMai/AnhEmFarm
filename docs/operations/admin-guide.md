# Seller guide

Sign in with an account provisioned by the admin CLI. Select **Admin** in the storefront to open the backend-owned seller workspace in a new tab. The workspace uses normal page navigation and HTML forms; every screen and mutation is authorized by the server. Use **Log out** in its sidebar when finished.

- **Dashboard and work queue:** Review order counts and the overdue queue. An order needs attention after it remains `PENDING` for 24 elapsed hours. The queue never changes order status automatically. Open an order to take the next valid action.
- **Orders:** New committed checkouts appear in the Orders list. Search by short order reference, recipient name/email, or carrier tracking number; combine search with status, COD collection, inclusive Vietnam-date, and Needs attention filters. Use Previous/Next for long result sets. Confirm, deliver by store or carrier, mark delivered, cancel with a reason, or record a return after physical receipt. Enter sellable restock quantities for returned items. COD collection is separate from delivery; corrections require a reason. Status changes queue owner-only email through the worker; use Email jobs to inspect delivery state.
- **Products and choices:** Create or edit products, categories, descriptions, status, formats, price and sale availability. Upload a JPEG, PNG, or WebP image up to 5 MB; the server validates and converts it. A product may have one optional single-select customer choice group. Give it a label such as “Sweetness” and enter one choice per line. Existing choices can be renamed or deactivated. Customers must choose one active option before adding a product that has an active group. Choices do not change price, and committed orders retain their original choice labels.
- **Inventory:** Record each adjustment with a reason. If a response is uncertain, reload stock and inspect the audit trail before making another adjustment.
- **Customers:** Search and page through accounts. Customer details show bounded order history; suspend or reactivate with a reason. Suspension invalidates existing sessions.
- **Settings and content:** Enter confirmed business details, delivery zones and fees, then review About, Contact, Shipping, Returns, Privacy and Terms pages. Publishing a page is explicit. Live sales require both the production `SALES_ENABLED` environment switch and seller confirmation of all launch prerequisites. Age-restricted products require separate configuration and review.
- **Reports:** Choose an inclusive Vietnam-date range. Delivered order value, COD collected and COD due are based on delivery or collection timestamps; they are not profit figures. Status totals are shown separately.
- **Audit and email jobs:** Review recent activity and delivery attempts. Diagnose SMTP failures before a controlled retry; do not copy private order or email payloads into tickets or logs.

Never use demo fixtures as production data. Tea and honey remain provisional until the owner confirms their details. The workspace is not a production-readiness claim: follow the [release checklist](release-checklist.md) before enabling live sales.
